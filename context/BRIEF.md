# Product brief

## One line
Ask anything about how loud or how dangerous a NYC street is, and watch the answer appear on a 3D map.

## Users
- Renters and home buyers checking a block before they move
- Parents and cyclists checking how safe a street is
- Journalists and community boards looking for hotspots

## Main features
1. **3D map:** an extruded view of NYC on OpenStreetMap. Column height shows how many incidents happened, and color shows how severe they were.
2. **AI chat:** questions in plain language get answered with real numbers from the data, and the map moves or filters to show the answer.

## Demo story (2 minutes)
1. Open on a tilted 3D view of all five boroughs, showing noise.
2. Type "Where is the loudest block in Bushwick on weekend nights?" The chat answers with a real count, and the map flies to that block and highlights it.
3. Switch to crashes. Type "Which Brooklyn intersections have the most cyclist injuries?" The chat lists the top results, and the map highlights them.
4. Click any column. A panel shows the breakdown, and you can choose "Ask about this block."

## Ground rules
- The chat never makes up numbers. Every figure comes from a data query.
- Show the data's limits in the app: 311 counts are what people reported, not measured noise, and crash data runs a few months behind.
