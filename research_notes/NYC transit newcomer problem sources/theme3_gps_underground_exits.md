## Key takeaways for the problem statement
- Official sources (GPS.gov, NIST) confirm GPS degrades near buildings and "indoor or underground use," and NIST says built-in phone indoor accuracy may be inadequate.
- Floor/level: phone barometers vary by device and are disturbed by HVAC/handling; Apple's own docs say floor level is not altitude. Subway depth/mezzanine specifically: no source found.
- Compass: Android docs confirm hard-iron bias and that rotation vectors without the magnetometer are immune to magnetic changes; no source found measuring compass error specifically in subway tunnels/trains or after surfacing.
- Connectivity: cell/Wi-Fi is in all 281 underground stations since Jan 2017, but tunnel coverage between stations is only a few segments as of Mar 2026 (2032 target uncertain).
- Exit confusion: strongest citable items are the 2007 NYC compass-sticker trial (AP/NBC), 2009 Exit Strategy "off by an entire avenue," and Time/AP on Apple exit directions. Most others are anecdotal or blogs.
