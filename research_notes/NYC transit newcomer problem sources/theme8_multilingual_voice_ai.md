- Local Law 245 of 2017 (identifying information) text itself not found; only agency reports and EO 34/41 sources. Do not cite the law's exact provisions.
- Not found: Pew-specific voice assistant data, CHI papers on low-literacy voice interfaces, 311/chatbot examples in other cities, Koenecke PNAS paper itself. Search quota ran out; a follow-up could cover these.
- MyCity chatbot was a business-focused tool; the Markup article does not mention multilingual features, so no evidence it was multilingual.

- Conflicts: NYC311 language count 175+ (2023 OTI) vs 170 (Probation page); Becker's 2% harm for Spanish vs a secondary blog's 28% (disregard).
