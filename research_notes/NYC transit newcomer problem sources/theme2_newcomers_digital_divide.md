# Theme 2: Newcomers, LEP people, older adults, and the digital divide in NYC transit

Verification tags in the third column: **fetched** = I opened the page and read the claim; **snippet** = URL came back from search and the claim comes from the search result text only (not opened; verify before quoting). Nothing here is invented.

## Takeaway
About 3.1M NYC residents are foreign-born (~38-40%); roughly half of them are limited English proficient (LEP). Older New Yorkers, especially immigrants with little schooling, are disproportionately offline at home. NYC's subway is mostly English-only for signs and announcements. Evidence on immigrants' transit barriers (language, app literacy, wayfinding, fear) is mostly from other cities/countries and is qualitative.
