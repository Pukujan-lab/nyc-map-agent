## Caveats for the report writer
- No source found directly proving "plain map with visible landmarks reduces stress in NYC subway"; this is an inference from landmark, load, and stress literature.
- Best counter-signals: landmarks on mobile maps can add load (Frontiers VR 2022); choice-overload effect is near zero in meta-analysis; crowding is not always perceived as stress (Cox review); stress-navigation findings are small-sample and lab-based.
- No independent rider-density data found for NYC; "crowding" is inferred from ridership counts.
- Some entries are secondary or weak (flagged). Wikipedia, consumer commute blogs and a Lynch essay site were seen but deliberately excluded.
