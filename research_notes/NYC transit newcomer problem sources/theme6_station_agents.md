Verification key: [F] = page fetched and read this session; [S] = seen only in search results/snippets (claim taken from snippet, not fully verified). Searches were run on 2026-10-24.

## 1. History of booth closures and agent cuts (2009 to 2021)

### Takeaway
Booths and agents were cut in waves from 2009 (about 300 agents cut by Oct 2009; 260 laid off plus 220 more threatened and 89 booths targeted in 2010). A TWU attorney says active booths fell from about 900 (2001) to about 400 (2026). A 2021 plan to close all 470 booths for lunch breaks was blocked in court.

## 3. Recent staffing disputes: closed booths when agents call out (2026)

### Takeaway
In 2026 NYC Transit began leaving booths locked for entire shifts when agents called out (Bulletin 001-26). TWU and disability advocates sued; on Sept 18, 2026 a judge voided the bulletin, finding it a partial station closure and disparately harmful to disabled riders. MTA says it will appeal.
