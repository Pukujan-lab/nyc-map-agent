## Takeaways
- NYC supply: ~1,000-1,100 public restrooms for ~8.8M residents (~1 per 7,700-8,000); ranked 93rd of 100 large cities (2019, Comptroller); council bill (passed Apr 2025) sets 1 per 2,000 by 2035.
- Reliability, not only count: Dec 2025 Council inspection found ~11% closed in posted hours and most open ones missing basics; MTA OIG found 5 of 32 sampled subway bathrooms closed and MTA poor at communicating location/status. This supports a "nearest OPEN restroom" framing.
- Subway: 125 bathrooms at 65 stations (OIG, Jan 2026) of ~472 stations; 133 had closed at COVID. Older counts (122, 128) conflict.
- ADA: 69% of park restrooms not ADA accessible (2019); "accessible" labels often unreliable (Ottawa: 63% had a barrier).
- Health/stress evidence is mostly from UK/Australia surveys and clinical studies; no NYC-specific study found.

## Gaps
- No NYC-specific survey of avoidance/stress from restroom scarcity; no peer-reviewed study directly quantifying travel avoidance from urinary incontinence (only case reports/qualitative).
- Pregnancy-specific restroom access evidence: none found.
- Riders Alliance-specific statements: not found. Google official statement on restroom listing accuracy: not found.
- Current exact count of open subway restrooms and numbers in the OIG/Council PDFs not read first-hand (PDF text extraction failed); relayed via news summaries.
- Intro 694-A signed/implementation status unverified; Intro number is as reported by search summary.
