# street-name-sign.glb

"Street Name Sign" from Grab3D's Urban Street Kit, https://grab3d.com/models/city/street-name-sign/
License: CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution required). Credited here anyway.
Unmodified GLB (4,054 triangles, 2K textures). Street names are drawn at runtime as
canvas-text decals over its blades by `src/lib/map/streetSigns.ts`.

## Hero landmarks

`landmarks/*.glb` (Empire State, Chrysler, One WTC, Statue of Liberty) are low-poly massing models authored in this repository by `scripts/make-landmarks.mjs` from published dimensions. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
